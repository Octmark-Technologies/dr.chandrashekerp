Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how the clinical practice of Dr. Chandrashekar P collects, uses, and protects patient and visitor information gathered through this portfolio website. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality of your personal and medical information in accordance with Indian healthcare regulations and ethical guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website to explore treatments or schedule appointments, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, and email address provided during appointment bookings.
  • Medical Context: Any preliminary queries regarding joint pain, robotic surgery, or medical history you submit voluntarily via appointment forms or WhatsApp consultation desks.
  • Usage Data: Anonymized analytical data (cookies, IP addresses, browser types) gathered to optimize page loading speeds and overall website performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for clinical coordination and patient support:

  • To schedule and manage consultations at the International Knee & Orthopaedic Centre (IKOC).
  • To respond to your inquiries regarding robotic knee replacement procedures or sports injuries.
  • To verify patient credentials for appointment booking platforms (such as Practo).

3. Third-Party Integration and Booking

Our website links directly to external appointment platforms (Practo) and clinical communication desks (WhatsApp). Any information you enter on these third-party platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their terms when booking.

4. Data Security & Medical Privacy

Your personal information and clinical queries are strictly confidential. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with external marketing agencies. Patient records, surgical details, and consultation notes are stored in secure clinical systems at IKOC in compliance with patient-confidentiality laws.

5. Consent & Disclaimers

By submitting information through this site, you consent to our team contacting you via call, WhatsApp, or email to coordinate your appointment. Under NMC guidelines, this site is purely for educational awareness; no digital communication constitutes a formal surgical diagnosis until a physical clinical evaluation is conducted.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, you may reach out to the coordinator desk at:
