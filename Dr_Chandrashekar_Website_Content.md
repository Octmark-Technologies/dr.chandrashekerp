**Extended bio:**
For over 15 years **[confirm]**, Dr. Chandrashekar P has dedicated his practice to restoring movement and quality of life for people living with joint pain and sports injuries. His work centres on the knee — from robotic and computer-assisted total knee replacement to meniscus repair, ligament reconstruction and cartilage preservation for younger patients.

His approach pairs advanced technology with a simple principle: every patient deserves a clear explanation, a considered plan, and care that treats them like family.

## 4. Robotic & Computer-Assisted Joint Replacement *(signature section)*

**Heading:** Precision you can plan for

Traditional knee replacement relies heavily on the surgeon's manual judgement in the operating theatre. Robotic and computer-assisted surgery adds a layer of measurement and planning — the joint is mapped in detail, the surgical plan is rehearsed digitally, and implant positioning is guided in real time during the procedure.

Dr. Chandrashekar works across a range of these systems, including active robotic platforms and AI-assisted navigation. In January 2026 he led **India's first AI-powered total knee replacement** using an imageless, real-time guidance system.

**What this can mean for patients** *(benefit-led, no guarantees):*
- More accurate implant alignment and sizing
- A surgical plan tailored to each patient's anatomy
- A minimally invasive, tissue-sparing approach where suitable
- A focus on faster, more comfortable recovery

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

**How is robotic knee replacement different from conventional surgery?**
It adds detailed pre-surgical planning and real-time guidance during the operation, which can improve the accuracy of implant positioning. Whether it suits you depends on your anatomy and overall health, decided after assessment.

**Am I too old for knee replacement?**
Age alone rarely rules it out. Overall health, bone quality and fitness for surgery matter more. Many older adults do well; a consultation is the best way to know.

**How long is recovery?**
Most people begin walking with support very soon after surgery and progress over the following weeks with physiotherapy. Recovery timelines differ by individual and procedure.

**Is the surgery painful?**
Modern pain-management protocols are used to keep you comfortable during and after surgery. Your team will explain what to expect.

**How long do knee implants last?**
Contemporary implants are designed to last many years. Longevity depends on activity, weight, bone health and how well the implant is positioned — one reason precision matters.

**Do you treat sports injuries as well as arthritis?**
Yes — alongside joint replacement, the practice handles ACL/PCL reconstruction, meniscus and cartilage injuries, and shoulder problems.

**Can I get help with insurance and cash-payment options?**
The team can guide you through insurance and self-pay options at the time of consultation.
